# Sources

Primary source included in this repo:

- **CBK Digital Credit Providers Regulations, 2022** — Legal Notice No. 46, Kenya Gazette Supplement No. 45 (18 March 2022). Public legal text, included at `data/cbk_dcp_regulations_2022.pdf`.

Secondary sources cited in the analysis (linked, not redistributed):

- **Central Bank of Kenya, Bank Supervision Annual Report 2023** — source for the 15.6% banking-sector NPL benchmark and the 32-licensed-DCP figure. Central Bank of Kenya, https://www.centralbank.go.ke
- **KCB Group PLC, Annual Report and Financial Statements for the Year Ended 31 December 2023** — source for KCB M-Pesa's balance-sheet context (Kenya loan book, personal/household loans, consumer lending rate). KCB Group, https://ke.kcbgroup.com
- **Tala, Impact Report 2023** — source for Tala's origination volume, customer base, and consumer-protection practices. Tala, https://tala.co
- **Tala, MoneyMarch 2023 Report** — source for Tala customer income, borrowing, and savings trends used in the ambition-execution scoring. Tala, https://tala.co
- **Tala, Emerging Market Back-To-School Report** — source for Tala loan-purpose data (education lending). Tala, https://tala.co/2023-back-to-school-report/
- **KIPPRA Policy Brief No. 26/2022-2023, "Addressing Digital Credit Burden in Kenya"** by Valentine Michuki — source for digital credit default rates, borrower demographics, and CBK regulatory history. Kenya Institute for Public Policy Research and Analysis, https://www.kippra.or.ke

Company-specific figures for 4G Capital, Pezesha, and Zenka (loan sizes, default rates, funding facilities) are drawn from each company's own impact reports and press coverage, cited inline in the benchmarking workbook's "Score Rationale" and "2025 Coda" tabs.
